# Privacy Policy — Laid Off by AI

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Laid Off by AI ("the game", "we") is a single-player satirical game. We collect as little as the game
needs to run its global leaderboard, the daily challenge and optional purchases. We do not show ads, we
do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

**Controller:** Kudret YILMAZ, Sancaktepe / İstanbul, Türkiye · Contact: kudretylmzzz@gmail.com

## 1. What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A random player ID** created when the game first connects (anonymous sign-in; no name, e-mail, phone or password) | To link your runs and scores to you | Our backend (Supabase, servers in the United Kingdom) |
| **Nickname** you choose (optional) | Shown publicly next to your scores on the leaderboard | Backend |
| **Runs you play while online**: difficulty, mode, the run's seed and game version, the in-game actions of the run, final score, weeks survived and the career line | To verify scores (the server replays every run) and to rank them | Backend |
| **Reports** you send about another player's nickname | To moderate nicknames | Backend |
| **Purchase records**: the store's transaction ID and product, linked to your player ID | To check that a purchase really happened before a supported run enters the leaderboard, and so each purchase supports only one run | Backend and RevenueCat (our purchase provider) |
| **Technical data** that any server receives (IP address, time of request) | Security and abuse prevention; kept in short-lived server logs | Backend and RevenueCat |

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives your
player ID and purchase history to validate receipts — see RevenueCat's privacy policy
(https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

**Stays on your device only:** your saved game, settings (sound, language), whether you have seen the
intro and the tutorial, and **reminder notifications** — these are scheduled locally on your phone; no
push token is created and nothing is sent to us.

## 2. Legal basis (EEA, UK, Turkey)
Running the game and the leaderboard you choose to use (performance of a contract); preventing cheating
and abuse (legitimate interest); purchases (contract and legal obligations). Reminder notifications need
your permission and can be turned off in the game's menu or the phone's settings.

## 3. Sharing
We share data only with the service providers that run the game for us: Supabase (database and server
functions) and RevenueCat (purchase validation), and Apple/Google as the app stores. Your nickname, scores
and career line are public on the leaderboard. We do not sell data.

## 4. Retention
Leaderboard data is kept while the leaderboard runs. Purchase records are kept permanently, because they
prevent one purchase from being claimed twice. Server logs are kept for a short period by our providers.
When your data is deleted (see below), your nickname, runs, scores and reports are removed; purchase
records are kept without your nickname where required to prevent fraud and by law.

## 5. Your rights
**Delete your data in the game:** on the nickname screen (Leaderboard → nickname), tap "Delete my data" at
the bottom and confirm. Your account, nickname, runs, scores and reports are deleted at once.

You can also ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to processing, by
writing to kudretylmzzz@gmail.com. Include the nickname you used. Under GDPR / UK GDPR / KVKK you may also complain to
your data protection authority.

## 6. Children
The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 7. Changes
We will update this page if the game starts collecting anything new and change the date above.
